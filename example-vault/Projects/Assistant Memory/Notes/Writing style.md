# Writing style

How Ines wants prose written for her: READMEs, design notes, messages to her team, and replies in chat.

- Short sentences and plain words. Say "use", not "leverage".
- No em dashes. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop instead.
- Lead with the answer, then the detail. One recommendation, not five options.
- British spelling in documents she publishes ("organise", "colour").
- Headings are sentence case, never Title Case.

**Why:** Her team reads a lot of generated text and has learned to skim anything that sounds like marketing copy.

**How to apply:** Draft, then delete every sentence that does not add a fact. Check for em dashes before sending.
