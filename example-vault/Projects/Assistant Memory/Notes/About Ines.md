# About Ines

Ines Varga is a backend engineer living in Porto, Portugal (Europe/Lisbon time zone). She works at Tidewell Logistics, a freight booking software company, on the billing and invoicing platform: mostly Python services, PostgreSQL, and the event pipeline that turns shipments into invoices.

## Background

- Eight years of backend work, the last three at Tidewell. Before that, payments integrations at a travel startup.
- Strongest in Python, SQL and data modelling. Comfortable with TypeScript, still learning Rust in her spare time.
- Runs a small home server for her own projects; see [[Home server facts]].

## Side projects

- [[Harbor Ledger]]: imports her bank and card exports into one categorised ledger.
- [[Kestrel]]: a Raspberry Pi in the garden that identifies bird calls.

## How she likes to work

- Wants the reasoning before the code: say what you are about to change and why, then change it.
- Prefers small, reviewable steps over one large rewrite. Details are in [[Code review preferences]].
- Writes in plain, direct English; see [[Writing style]].
